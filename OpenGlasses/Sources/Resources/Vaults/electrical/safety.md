# Safety — Electrical Work

These are a starting point. Replace or extend them with your organisation's own safety rules, in your own words.

## Before opening any panel or enclosure

- Treat every conductor as live until you have proved it dead.
- Isolate the supply at its source, then lock it off and tag it under your organisation's lockout procedure. Keep your own key.
- Prove dead: check the tester on a known live source, test every conductor you will touch (each line to every other, and each to neutral and to earth or ground), then check the tester on the known source again.
- Look for a second supply before you trust the first isolation: a backfeed, a generator, a solar or battery inverter, a UPS, a shared neutral, a capacitor that holds a charge.

## While you work

- Wear the protective equipment and keep the distances your organisation and your jurisdiction set for the task. This starter does not state them.
- Do not work on or near live parts unless your organisation's procedure allows that task and you are trained, authorised and equipped for it. If in doubt, stop and escalate.

## Ratings, sizes and limits

- Never guess a rating, a conductor size, a device setting or a limit. Read it from the nameplate, the drawing, or your organisation's loaded code book, and cite the rule or table it came from.
- A value the assistant reads from a table comes with its table, row, column and page. Check it against the printed page before you rely on it.

## Before you re-energise

- Remove tools and test equipment, refit every cover, and tell everyone the work affects.
- Remove locks and tags only as your lockout procedure says, then restore the supply and test the circuit.
