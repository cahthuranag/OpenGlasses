# Synthetic manual upload fixtures

`manual-upload.doc` and `manual-upload.docx` are exports of invented workshop text, generated with
macOS `textutil` from UTF-8 input. They contain a sump-plug value of 37 Nm and a fictional A1/F7
circuit. They contain no customer documents, signing material, macros, or personal metadata.

They exercise real Word-export byte layouts rather than a parser-specific hand-built container.
