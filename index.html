What it does

Say it. See it. Get it done.

One assistant for what is in front of you, what was said, and what needs doing next.

See

Understands what you are looking at

Read a sign, ask about a piece of equipment, or read contact details from a business card.

“What does this label say?”

Talk

A live conversation, in your language

Ask a quick question or settle in. Translate spoken language and follow along with captions.

“Translate what she is saying.”

Remember

Keeps the details that matter

Where you parked, notes about people and places, and meeting notes you can ask about later.

“What did we agree on Tuesday?”

How memory and the brain work

Act

Does the next thing for you

Calendar, reminders, directions, music and the lights, plus Home Assistant, Siri Shortcuts and MCP tool servers.

“Turn on the workshop lights.”

Choose

Runs on the AI you pick

A cloud model, a model on your own server, or a compatible local model on your device. Route different requests to different models.

“Use my own server for this.”

Offline

Keeps going without a signal

With the on-device setup—local AI, speech recognition and voice—the conversation runs offline on your device.

“No signal? Carry on.”

Accessibility

Hear text. Ask about what is around you.

Reading Assistant, spoken descriptions, captions and voice controls, with no separate accessibility unlock.

Begin with your iPhone or iPad, headphones or AirPods, or compatible glasses. Our accessibility page explains the features, setup, and the independent-use validation still needed.

Explore accessibility and help shape what comes next.

Where it lives

One assistant. More ways to use it.

Start on iPhone or iPad. Add headphones or AirPods, or reach the assistant from your Apple Watch or compatible glasses.

1

Your iPhone or iPad

Use the app on an iPhone with iOS 26 or later, or an iPad with iPadOS 26 or later, camera included.

2

Your headphones

Hear replies through headphones or AirPods connected to your iPhone or iPad.

3

Your wrist

Start a conversation from your Apple Watch, paired with your iPhone, without reaching for the phone.

4

Your glasses

Compatible Meta smart glasses connect through your iPhone to add hands-free camera and audio.

Private by design

No account. No analytics. No one in the middle.

It stays on your device

Conversation history, memories, notes and recordings are stored on the device running Avenkin.

It goes only where you send it

Data leaves your device only when a feature needs an outside service, such as the AI provider you picked.

This site keeps the same promise

No cookies, no analytics and nothing loaded from anyone else. Read the privacy policy.
